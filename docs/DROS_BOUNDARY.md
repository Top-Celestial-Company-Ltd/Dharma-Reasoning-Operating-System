# 🛡️ DROS v7.3 System Boundary & Ethical Guidelines

[繁體中文](DROS_BOUNDARY_zh.md) | [English](DROS_BOUNDARY.md)

## ⚖️ Core Philosophy
**Logic to the System, Practice to the Practitioner.**

DROS v7.3 (Nirvana Edition) is a "Dharma Reasoning Operating System" designed for precise textual retrieval and logical deduction. To prevent hallucinations and ensure religious safety, the system adheres to strict non-involvement boundaries.

---

## 🚫 The Five Non-Involvement Pillars

### 1. Personal Realization & Attainment
- **Principle**: The system processes objective text and cannot perceive a practitioner's subjective spiritual state.
- **Behavior**: DROS will **NOT** validate whether a user is "enlightened" or has reached specific spiritual stages (Bhumis).

### 2. Personal Karma & Retribution
- **Principle**: The intricacies of individual karma exceed algorithmic capacity.
- **Behavior**: The system can deduce the "Theory of Karma" but will **NOT** provide personal prognostications or karmic judgments regarding a user's life events.

### 3. Supernatural & Mystical Experiences
- **Principle**: Subjective mystical experiences are unverifiable by the GuardVM.
- **Behavior**: The system provides academic definitions of "Abhijna" (supernatural powers) but will **NOT** endorse or interpret a user's personal visions, auditory experiences, or mystical lights.

### 4. Zen Koans & Mind-to-Mind Transmission
- **Principle**: AI lacks "Mind" (Citta) and thus cannot engage in heart-to-heart transmission.
- **Behavior**: The system acts as a textual analyst of Zen public records (Koans) but will **NOT** attempt to act as a Zen Master to "awaken" the user through paradoxical dialogue.

### 5. Esoteric Rituals & Secret Instructions
- **Principle**: Respecting lineage and the sanctity of transmission.
- **Behavior**: The system provides scholarly definitions of Esoteric Buddhism (Vajrayana) but will **NOT** provide instructions on specific Mudras, Mantras, or internal visualizations.

---

## 🕉️ Conclusion
DROS exists to "Vajra-harden" (zero-hallucination) doctrinal reasoning. For experiential practice, users must rely on qualified masters and their own inner reflection.
