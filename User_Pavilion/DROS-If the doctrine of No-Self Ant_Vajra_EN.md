## Doctrinal Analysis

Based on the current DROS authorized repository, a Vajra deduction can be performed.

The doctrinal framework presented to resolve the apparent paradox between the principle of No-Self (*Anātman*) and the continuity of karmic consequence (*karmaphala*) across lifetimes is predicated on the function of the *Ālayavijñāna* [阿賴耶識], or the "storehouse consciousness." This concept provides a mechanism for karmic inheritance without positing a permanent, unchanging soul or self (*Ātman*).

The *Ālayavijñāna* is posited as the fundamental, eighth consciousness, underlying the six sensory consciousnesses (visual, auditory, olfactory, gustatory, tactile) and the seventh, the afflicted mind (*kliṣṭa-manas*). It is characterized as a subliminal, ever-flowing stream (*srotas*) of consciousness that serves as the receptacle (*ādāna*) for the latencies or "seeds" (*bīja*) of all volitional actions.

The mechanics are as follows:

1.  **Imprinting of Seeds (*bīja*)**: Every action, whether physical, verbal, or mental, imprints a karmic seed (*bīja*) into this consciousness stream. This process is known as "perfuming" (*vāsanā*). These seeds are not static entities but potentialities carried within the flow of the *Ālayavijñāna*.

2.  **Continuity without Permanence**: The *Ālayavijñāna* itself is subject to moment-to-moment arising and ceasing (*kṣaṇika*), thus upholding the principle of impermanence (*Anitya*). It is a dynamic causal continuum, not a static entity. The continuity is one of cause and effect—a stream of karmic information—not the transmigration of a fixed, unchanging self.

3.  **Maturation of Seeds (*vipāka*)**: When causal conditions (*pratyaya*) are met, these dormant seeds ripen (*vipāka*), manifesting as subjective experiences and objective circumstances in the present or a future life. The *Ālayavijñāna* is thus also referred to as the "maturation consciousness" (*vipāka-vijñāna*).

4.  **The Mechanism of Rebirth**: At the dissolution of the physical aggregates (*skandha*) at death, the *Ālayavijñāna* stream, laden with the totality of unripened karmic seeds, persists. It is this karmically charged stream that grasps a new substratum of existence (*āśraya*), thereby initiating a new life process conditioned by the karmic potencies it carries.

Crucially, the *Ālayavijñāna* is an impersonal, causally determined process. It is devoid of an inherent self or ego. Therefore, the doctrine of *Ālayavijñāna* provides a sophisticated model for rebirth and karmic inheritance that operates in full compliance with the foundational tenets of *Anātman* (No-Self) and *Anitya* (Impermanence).
